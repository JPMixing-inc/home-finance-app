HOME FINANCE - SETUP
====================

Home Finance is a household budget app that runs on your own computer. Your
data stays on that computer. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on bank
syncing (Plaid) or the AI features (Anthropic), and both use keys you add
yourself. Phones and other computers on your Wi-Fi can use it too, while the
app is open.


1. INSTALL
----------
  Mac:      Open the .dmg file and drag Home Finance into Applications.
            The first time you open it, macOS may say it "cannot be opened"
            or is "from an unidentified developer". Click Done, then go to
            System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
            "Open Anyway". You only do this once.

  Windows:  Right-click the zip, choose "Extract All", and keep the
            "Home Finance" folder somewhere such as Documents. Open it and
            double-click "Home Finance.exe". To pin it, right-click the exe
            and choose "Pin to Start" or "Send to > Desktop (create
            shortcut)".
            If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears, click
            "More info", then "Run anyway".
            When Windows asks about the firewall, allow "Private networks".
            That lets your phone connect.


2. MAKE YOUR LOGIN
------------------
The first time it opens, Home Finance asks you to create a username and
password. You can add logins for others in your household later, under
Settings.

Then add your accounts and import bank exports (CSV, OFX or QFX) from the
Import page.


3. USE IT ON YOUR PHONE
-----------------------
Open Settings > Phone access (or choose Tools > Phone Access in the menu).
Scan the QR code with your phone's camera, or type the address shown, which
looks like http://192.168.1.20:8090. Sign in with the same login.

On an iPhone, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen" to get an app icon.

Your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi, and Home Finance has to be open on the
computer. Minimizing the window is fine; quitting stops phone access too. If
you only want to use it on this computer, click "Turn off phone access" on
the same page.


4. OPTIONAL EXTRAS
------------------
  Automatic bank sync   Uses your own Plaid account (the free Trial plan
                        covers up to 10 banks). Paste the keys on the
                        Bank sync page.
  AI features           Uses your own Anthropic (Claude) API key. Paste it
                        in Settings.

Step-by-step guides for both are at:
  https://github.com/JPMixing-inc/home-finance-app#bank-sync-with-plaid


5. WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES
------------------------
Choose Tools > Show Data Folder in the app, or go to:
  Mac:      ~/Library/Application Support/Home Finance
  Windows:  %APPDATA%\Home Finance

finance.db holds everything. To back it up, use Settings > "Download a
backup", or copy that folder while the app is closed.

To move to a new computer, install Home Finance there, open it once, quit
it, and copy your old data folder over the new one.

Updating the app never touches your data folder.
