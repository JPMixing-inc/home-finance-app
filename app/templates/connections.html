Set up Plaid

Bank sync uses Plaid. You log into your bank in Plaid's own window, so your bank password never touches this server. Plaid gives us read-only transactions and balances.

  1. Sign up at dashboard.plaid.com and choose personal use when asked. The free Trial plan allows 10 bank logins.
  2. In the Plaid dashboard open Developers → Keys.
  3. Paste the client_id and the Production secret below. (To try it with a fake bank first, use the Sandbox secret and pick Sandbox.)
